About
DJ Garvin

DJ journey: Originally from Edo State, Nigeria, DJ Garvin began his DJ journey in 2002, building his early experience and passion for music before taking a short break after two years. His passion for music never faded, and after relocating to the United Kingdom, he returned to DJing and has since built approximately 3–4 years of continuous professional experience in the UK entertainment scene.

Versatility & professionalism: DJ Garvin is known for his versatility, professionalism, and ability to create the right atmosphere for every occasion. His work spans across the UK, with a strong presence in London, Manchester, West Yorkshire, and other locations.

Events & live entertainment: Over the years, DJ Garvin has performed at a wide range of events, including weddings, marriage ceremonies, birthdays, private parties, corporate functions, formal dinners, award nights, club events, concerts, festivals, open-air entertainment, cultural celebrations, social gatherings, and live artist performances. He also provides DJ support for artists and live entertainment, helping create an energetic and engaging experience for audiences.

Supporting artists & events: Beyond performing, DJ Garvin is passionate about supporting upcoming artists and creating opportunities within the entertainment industry. He is also involved in organising and hosting events, bringing together DJs, artists, entertainers, and audiences through carefully planned entertainment experiences.

Nigerian & UK connection: With a strong connection to both Nigerian and UK entertainment culture, DJ Garvin continues to grow his brand while using music and events to connect people, support emerging talent, and deliver memorable experiences wherever he performs.
